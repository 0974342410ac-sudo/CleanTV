# CEC keys, tried on .150 with an FPT Play 650 box on HDMI1 (2026-10-31): MTvPlayer's RootActivity hands every key to TvCecManager.sendCecKey and keeps those it accepts. Its table has Left/Right/Up/Back and ENTER (66, sent as CEC Select) but not DPAD_CENTER (23), so the remote's OK never reached the box. MStar also sends no CEC release: Android boxes hold the key until their 550 ms timeout, which made OK a long press (our launcher's tile then opened app details). Launcher 2.13 (HdmiCecKey, in HomeKeyService) sends ENTER on OK down and MEDIA_PLAY on OK up to end the hold; TV360's tile then opened normally. Side effect: in a player where OK pauses, Play may resume at once. The whole sendable table (libcecmanager_jni + tvos): 4 Exit, 19-22 arrows, 24/25 volume, 66 Select, 86 Stop, 89/90 rew/ff, 91/164 mute, 126 play, 127 pause. After the launcher restarts, the service knows the front window only from the next window change. "input keyevent" does not go through the accessibility filter: to test, use root and sendevent /dev/input/event7 1 28 1 (Bluetooth remote, OK), then 0 0 0, 1 28 0, 0 0 0. Volume keys never go to CEC here: they change the projector's own volume on HDMI too (seen 28-30), and the box sends no CEC volume: its remote changes the box's own volume bar (seen 3 to 12) while tvos logs no UI_PRESS from it. Its Settings > Hien thi va am thanh > HDMI-CEC has only the on/off switch (on). Nothing on the projector can catch those presses; incoming UI_PRESS handling in tvos stays untested. The owner chose not to relay volume over the network. The onn 4K box (.147) is different: its volume keys do go out as CEC (80 44 41/42, with release). tvos logs them ("Pass through remote control key 61516") and stops there: no Android key, and TvCecManager's listener has no such event. Launcher 2.13 (HdmiCecVolume) follows tvos's log while a player window is in front and calls adjustStreamVolume; seen on .150 from "input keyevent 24/25" on .147 (VolumeCommand and TvService follow; system volume_music does not, it stays 35).
